Create a [aspect ratio, default 16:9] storyboard preview sheet with [panel count] panels covering the full P## sequence for [one-line scene premise].

[HEADER]
"[cinematic title, 2-5 words]"
"[one compact sequence-goal line]"

[BOARD STRUCTURE]
AUTO layout, [panel count] panels. Compact panel headers: `P## / shot tag / beat name`.

[VISUAL STYLE]
Panel interiors are silent, sparse, open-outline blocking thumbnails: outer body-mass and limb contours only, thin uniform linework, broad negative space, no internal anatomy, no face detail, no clothing/material detail, no color, no shading beyond flat outline. These panels exist to validate camera angle, shot scale, framing, pose, screen direction, and spatial layout only — they carry no final identity, style, or color information. That information lives in the reference plates and the video prompts, not here.

[CONTINUITY]
Keep entity count, screen position, and spatial geography consistent across panels. Each panel is one frozen instant — no before/after wording, no combined time states for one entity in one panel.

[COUNT LOCK — omit if not count-sensitive]
Every panel preserves exactly [count + role names] as one instance each. A fallen/dropped/broken/changed entity is the same entity continuing, not a duplicate.

[TEXT RULES]
Visible text: only the two quoted header lines and the compact panel headers. No other labels, arrows, icons, or annotations inside panels.

[CONSTRAINTS]
No color, no finished illustration detail, no duplicate/ghost entities, no visible internal IDs, no logos or watermarks beyond the sheet's own header design.

[BEATS]
One panel per P##, using the panel-writing pattern from references/craft-guide.md:
P01: [camera angle + shot scale + composition + role silhouette pose + screen position + object/effect state + spatial relation + visible result]
P02: [...]
