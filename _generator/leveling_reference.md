# Royalti Studios — Reading Level Reference
Primary labels: **Lexile band + Guided Reading (GR) letter** on every children's/leveled pack cover.

| Band | Lexile | GR Letter | Grade | Writing constraints for generated stories |
|------|--------|-----------|-------|-------------------------------------------|
| Emergent | BR-160L | A-C | PreK-K | 1 short sentence per page (3-6 words), heavy pattern/repetition, picture-carried meaning, sight words only, 30-100 words total |
| Early | 160L-310L | D-G | K-1 | 1-2 sentences per page, simple SVO sentences (4-8 words), decodable phonics focus (CVC, blends), 100-250 words |
| Transitional | 310L-420L | H-K | 1-2 | 2-4 sentences per page, simple + compound sentences, early chapters OK, dialogue introduced, 250-600 words |
| Developing | 420L-650L | L-P | 2-3 | Short chapters, sentences mostly 6-10 words (max 14), Tier 2 vocab introduced with context + repetition, 600-1,500 words |
| Fluent | 650L-850L | Q-T | 3-5 | Full chapter books, varied sentence structure, subplots allowed, figurative language with support, 4,000-15,000 words |
| Advanced | 850L-1010L | U-W | 5-6 | Middle grade complexity, multiple POV possible, thematic depth, 20,000-45,000 words |
| YA | 1010L+ | X-Z+ | 7-12 | Full YA craft; content gates matter more than syntax |

## Rules for "real stories at level" packs
1. **Level lock first.** Every leveled pack's Prompt 1 locks the Lexile band, GR letter, and grade — all later prompts inherit it.
2. **Constraint block in every writing prompt.** Sentence length, word count, vocab tier, and repetition rules are pasted into the writing prompts themselves, not just stated once.
3. **QA at level.** Each pack includes a verification prompt: re-check the finished story against the band (sentence lengths, syllable counts, new-word count, decodability) and revise anything over level.
4. **Real story, not worksheet.** Stories must have a character, a want, a problem, and a satisfying resolution — leveling constrains the language, never the storytelling.
5. **Content gates by age:** no romance below YA; peril age-appropriate; themes match band (friendship/family for A-K, growing independence L-T, identity/justice U+).

## Cover labeling format
`Lexile 420L-650L  |  Guided Reading Level L-P  |  Grades 2-3`
